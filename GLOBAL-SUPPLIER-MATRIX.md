## Strategy

**Current state:** PrintKK (China) is the only active supplier. Ships globally in 15-25 days.

**Target state:** One verified supplier per region/nation that can:
1. Print our custom designs on stretch polyester hood covers (dye sublimation)
2. Ship to local customers within 3-7 business days
3. Accept orders via Shopify integration, API, or manual forwarding

**Reality check:** Car hood covers are a niche product. No major POD platform (Printful, Printify, Gelato, Prodigi, Merchize) carries them. Most in-country suppliers will be general sublimation/textile printers who would need to source or manufacture the base hood cover (elastic polyester, 63"x47"). The outreach emails must confirm whether they can do cut-and-sew on this specific product — not just fabric printing.
