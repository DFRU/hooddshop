**Option B: Image-to-video (recommended — better flag accuracy)**

Step 1 — Generate the key frame in Midjourney:
```
A glossy black sedan in a dimly lit parking lot at night, hood facing camera,
with vivid sky blue and white horizontal stripes and a golden sun emblem
stretched across the entire hood. Single overhead spotlight illuminating the
hood. Young adults in streetwear crowding around the car, one filming with a
phone. Sky blue underglow accent lights on neighboring cars. Cinematic,
shallow depth of field, cool color grading. --ar 9:16 --v 6.1 --style raw
```

Step 2 — Feed the Midjourney image into Kling img2vid with this motion prompt:
```
Camera slowly pushes in toward the car hood while people gesture excitedly
around it. One person raises their phone to film. Slight camera shake adds
energy. Lens flare from spotlight. 8 seconds.
```
